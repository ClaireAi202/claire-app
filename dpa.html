CClaire

Data processing agreement

Duffel Music ApS · Last updated 23 November 2026

This agreement applies between the customer using Claire with their e-conomic agreement (the "Controller") and Duffel Music ApS (the "Processor"), in accordance with GDPR article 28.

1. Subject and purpose

The Processor processes personal data in the Controller's e-conomic agreement only to provide Claire: showing overviews, creating invoice drafts, and booking and sending invoices that the Controller approves.

2. Data and data subjects

Customers' and contacts' names, addresses, e-mail addresses, VAT/CVR numbers, invoice lines and payment information. Data subjects are the Controller's customers, contacts and users.

3. Instructions

The Processor only processes data on the Controller's documented instructions, which are these terms and the actions users take in Claire. The Processor tells the Controller if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.

4. Confidentiality and security

  • Only people at the Processor who need access have it, and they are bound by confidentiality.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by the hosting provider.
  • Access keys are stored in protected script properties. Users sign in with one-time codes.
  • Each customer's data is kept separate and is only shown to that customer's users and the Processor.

5. Sub-processors

The Controller approves the following sub-processors: Google (hosting via Google Workspace and Apps Script) and Anthropic (Claude API, for the AI assistant). The Processor gives at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor, and the Controller may object.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Transfers to countries outside the EU/EEA only take place on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

7. Assistance and breaches

The Processor helps the Controller answer requests from data subjects and meet its obligations. The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay, and at the latest within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

8. Retention and deletion

Data fetched from e-conomic is cached for up to 6 hours. When the collaboration ends, the Processor deletes all of the Controller's data within 30 days, and the Controller revokes Claire's access in e-conomic.

9. Audit

The Processor makes available the information needed to show compliance with this agreement and allows reasonable audits.

10. Term and law

This agreement applies as long as the Processor processes data for the Controller and is governed by Danish law. Contact: oliver@duffelmusic.com.
