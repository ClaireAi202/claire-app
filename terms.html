Claire is a private management dashboard operated by Duffel Music ApS ("we"). It is offered only to artists and producers managed by Duffel Music ApS and to people we invite. These terms apply when you use Claire, including its connection to e-conomic.

1. What Claire does

Claire shows your track catalogue, producer agreements, royalty data and finances in one place. When you connect your e-conomic agreement, Claire can show invoices, customers and payments, and create invoices as drafts in e-conomic. An invoice is only booked and sent through e-conomic when a user approves it in Claire. The built-in AI assistant can prepare drafts and answer questions, but never books or sends anything on its own.
