Privacy policy

Duffel Music ApS · Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Duffel Music ApS handles personal data in Claire. Duffel Music ApS is data controller for user accounts and acts as data processor for the data in your e-conomic agreement (see our data processing agreement).

What we process

  • Account data: name, e-mail address, role and sign-in history.
  • Catalogue and agreement data: tracks, artists, labels, fees and producer agreements that you or we enter.
  • e-conomic data (only when you connect e-conomic): customers, invoices, products, booked entries and accounting years.
  • Questions to the AI assistant and the data needed to answer them.

Why we process it

Only to provide Claire: show overviews, create and send invoices you approve, remind you about missing invoices, and answer your questions. The legal basis is our agreement with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in running a secure service (art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it for us

  • Google (Google Workspace and Apps Script) hosts Claire and stores catalogue data and account data.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) generates the AI assistant's answers. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Visma e-conomic holds your accounting data. Claire reads and writes to it only through the access you grant.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. Where that happens, transfers rely on the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Data fetched from e-conomic is only kept in a temporary cache for up to 6 hours. It is not copied into Claire's own storage.
  • Account and catalogue data is kept while you use Claire and deleted within 30 days after the collaboration ends, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access keys are stored in protected script properties, never in spreadsheets or code. Sign-in requires a one-time code, and each producer only sees their own data.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of your data, and object to processing. Write to oliver@duffelmusic.com. You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk).
